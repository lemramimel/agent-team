### Findings (fixed in #13; 1, 5 confirmed by replay)
1. Atlas passed `model` and `effort` in 1 run out of 4. Fixed: the agent files carry their tier's values and Atlas passes them only to deviate (Vera at `medium` on `risk:low`).
2. The graphify proposal had no memory. Fixed: `methods-check.sh decide graphify enabled|declined|later` writes to `team/methods.json`, and `graphify-gate.sh` prints the decision.
3. S10 expected Sally's note before Iris while `commands/feature.md` makes the design step optional. Fixed: the note is required only when the design step ran; scenario S10 and the prompts say so.
4. Briefs written with a shell heredoc were refused, and `team/PROTOCOL.md` was absent from projects. Fixed: briefs use the Write tool, and `/team-init` copies `PROTOCOL.md` and `ROSTER.md` into the project (existing projects re-run `/team-init`).
5. The role in parentheses was written on the first mention only. Fixed in the prompts with a wrong and a right example and a reread before sending; Confirmed by two replays (S14 passed).
6. Iris repeated the primary color as a raw `rgba` for a shadow. Fixed: derived colors use `color-mix` or an existing token.

7. In the `risk:medium` run with "no question" in the prompt, Atlas wrote "j'ai enregistré la réponse « plus tard »" for BMAD, although the user had answered nothing. Not fixed: `agents/atlas.md` and `commands/feature.md` should say that a proposal left unanswered is not recorded, and that Atlas never records an answer the user did not give.
8. The `m1` process printed its final result and then did not exit; Atlas had called `ScheduleWakeup` once in that run. Not diagnosed: one sample, to watch.
